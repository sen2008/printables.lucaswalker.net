About

Woodland Printables is a small one person project from Michigan. I make cozy woodland printables: nursery wall art, simple coloring pages for little kids, and complete baby shower kits, all in the same soft watercolor style.

The watercolor animal artwork is generated with AI illustration tools, then curated, cleaned up, and composed into finished printable designs by hand. Every kit and page is put together and checked by a human before it goes up.

Questions? The fastest way to reach me is through the Ko-fi shop page.
