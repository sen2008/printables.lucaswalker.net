Woodland baby shower printable kits

One woodland critter, one complete party. Each kit is a coordinated set of 45 printable files plus a bonus bingo card generator, all in the same soft watercolor style as the nursery art above. Everything is instant download, nothing ships.

What is inside every kit

  • Stationery: fillable invitation, thank you card, book request and diaper raffle inserts
  • Games: 20 unique bingo cards plus 15 more game sheets (word scramble, baby predictions, nursery rhyme quiz, and more) with answer key
  • Decor: welcome sign, table signs, food tent cards, full A to Z letter banner
  • Favors and tags: favor tags, gift tags, water bottle wraps, cupcake toppers
  • Bonus: an HTML bingo generator that makes unlimited unique cards in your browser, offline

Printing tips

  • Cardstock (65 to 110 lb) for invitations, signs, and game cards.
  • Standard copy paper is fine for game sheets guests will write on.
  • Print the banner on regular paper; it is designed to fold over string.
